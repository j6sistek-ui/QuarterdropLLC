Remember what a quarter could start?

A new world. A close call. A reason to try again. Quarterdrop takes its name from that simple arcade ritual: one quarter in, one more round.

We’re a small independent game studio based in Novi, Michigan, bringing that spirit to new adventures—from Acornaut’s colorful flights to the unpredictable frontier of Space Survival.
