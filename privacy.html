Privacy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy covers this website, quarterdropgames.com. Game-specific privacy information is separate from this website policy.

Who we are

This site is run by Quarterdrop LLC, a Michigan limited liability company based in Novi, Michigan. "We" and "us" mean Quarterdrop LLC.

What this site collects

There are no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising or tracking run by us on this site. Fonts, images, video, styles and scripts are served from this domain. The animation controls use temporary page state only; we do not store your selection or send it anywhere.

External links

Our game links take you to other websites, including Acornaut and itch.io. We do not load an itch.io embed on this site. If you follow a link, the destination website handles your visit under its own privacy policy, including the itch.io privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers record ordinary request information, such as your IP address, the time of the request and your browser type, to deliver pages and protect the service. We do not see or use those logs. GitHub handles them under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your message and email address and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any list or share your message with anyone.
